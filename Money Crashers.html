EarnIn Review – Free Paycheck Advances if You Skip the Fee and the Tip

Andrew Schrage November 12, 2026November 12, 2026
Money Crashers Rating
4.1/5 · Good
Learn More

EarnIn

  • Best for: Workers who can wait a day or two for a free advance
  • What Sets It Apart: As much as $1,000 a pay period with no monthly fee and no direct-deposit switch
  • Loan Amounts: Up to $200 a day and $1,000 a pay period ($150 a day in Maryland); new members’ daily limit averages $85
  • Terms: Repaid by automatic withdrawal on payday; no collections or credit reporting if a repayment fails
  • Features: Free delivery in 1 to 2 business days; Lightning Speed within 30 minutes
  • Monthly Fee: $0
  • Rates: No interest
  • Cost: Lightning Speed $3.99 up to $75, $5.99 up to $150, $8.99 above (5%, $3.99 minimum, in FL, IL, IN, OH, PA and TX; $4.99 to $9.99 in CO and WI); optional tip; no late fees
  • Credit Needed: None; no credit check
  • Eligibility: A regular direct-deposit paycheck of $320 or more a pay period and a verifiable work email or location; not in Connecticut
  • Pros: Free with standard delivery and a $0 tip; no monthly fee; no collections
  • Cons: Steep instant fees on small advances; Cash Out Link members pay $1.99 each; payday withdrawals can overdraw you
Just the Tip:

Use EarnIn if your paycheck arrives by direct deposit on a regular schedule and you can wait a day or two for your money. Its Cash Out advances cost nothing when you choose the free standard transfer and set the optional tip to $0. Instant delivery costs $3.99 or more.

EarnIn lets workers cash out part of the pay they’ve already earned before payday, then takes the money back from their checking account when the paycheck lands.

Key Features

  • Up to $200 a day and $1,000 a pay period, or $150 a day in Maryland
  • Lower limits at first, with an $85 average daily limit for new members and an increase within two pay periods for most, EarnIn says
  • Free delivery in 1 to 2 business days, or within 30 minutes through Lightning Speed for $3.99 to $8.99 in most states
  • No monthly fee, interest, or credit check, and an optional tip that can be $0
  • Repaid by an automatic withdrawal from your checking account on payday

Pros

  • Costs nothing if you take the standard transfer and tip $0
  • No need to move your direct deposit to EarnIn
  • No collections or credit reporting if a repayment fails
  • Tips refunded on request within 30 days of repayment

Cons

  • $5.99 for instant delivery of a $100 advance, 6% of the amount
  • Needs a regular direct-deposit paycheck of at least $320 a pay period and a work email or work location EarnIn can verify
  • A payday withdrawal that can overdraw your account, with the bank’s fee yours to pay
  • $1.99 per advance for members using Cash Out Link, an overdraft account at Lead Bank
  • A tip or a fee on more than 92% of its Colorado advances from January 2023 to July 2025, the state’s attorney general says in a lawsuit

Workers paid by direct deposit on a steady schedule can cover a one-time gap here for $0, as long as they wait a day or two and skip the tip. Gig workers with uneven deposits, and anyone who would cash out every payday, do better with a credit union’s payday alternative loan, which can be repaid over several months instead of in one payment on payday.
