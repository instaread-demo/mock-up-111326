Simian Scandal at Ole Miss

Kevin DeAnna and Paul Kersey, American Renaissance, November 12, 2026

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

Some white girls made monkey noises at an apartment door. Who cares? Yet this trivial incident is now a national news story. It illustrates a larger point. Even “conservative” institutions are eager to surrender, and there is no point in defending them out of nostalgia and wishful thinking. Instead, the Right, and the remnants of the white South, need to cynically get what they can out of these collapsing organizations rather than trying to save them from themselves.
